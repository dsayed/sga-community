About Us

The Right Dog For The Right Home

Since 2007, Saving Great Animals has been Seattle's trusted all-breed dog rescue. We believe every dog deserves a second chance, and every family deserves to find their perfect companion. Our foster-based model means every dog gets individual attention and love while they wait for their forever home.

  • All-breed rescue
  • Foster-based program
  • Comprehensive training resources
  • Community of 200+ volunteers
